# Atlas EV - Intelligent EV Charging Assistant

In order to assist owners of electric vehicles in finding dependable charging stations, estimating wait times, comparing charging costs, and choosing the best charging choice based on real-time parameters, Atlas EV is an intelligent EV charging recommendation platform.

By offering consumers intelligent suggestions rather than requiring them to manually browse across numerous charging networks, the platform seeks to lessen charging uncertainty and range anxiety.

--- 

# Problem Description

Despite the quick uptake of electric vehicles, consumers continue to encounter a number of difficulties:

Prolonged lines at charging stations
Absence of information about the availability of chargers in real timeSeveral billing applications with disjointed data
Comparing billing costs might be challenging.Anxiety about range when traveling long distances
Uncertainty over the dependability of chargers

---

# Fix

A centralized platform offered by Atlas EV assists EV owners in making wiser charging choices via:

Finding a nearby EV charging station
Recommendations for intelligent charging stations
A projection of the estimated waiting time
Reliability score for chargers
Comparing the costs of several stations
Community-based evaluations and rankings
